Audit & Assurance Services

We provide a comprehensive range of audit and assurance services to ensure regulatory compliance, enhance transparency in financial reporting, and provide the mandatory professional endorsement of facts and figures required by authorities.

Our Audit & Assurance Portfolio

  • Statutory Audit: Ensuring timely compliance with audits mandated under the Companies Act, LLP Act, and Societies Registration Act.
  • Internal Audit: Evaluating internal control systems to identify inefficiencies and improve overall operational effectiveness.
  • Concurrent Audit: Ongoing monitoring of operations to provide timely insights into business performance and regulatory compliance for proactive decision-making.
  • Compliance Audit: Equipped to conduct audits specifically mandated by regulators under current law.
  • Due Diligence: Tailored exercises including entity acquisition reviews and pre-scrutiny of records to address deficiencies before statutory or third-party inspections.
  • Physical Verification Audits: Comprehensive support for the physical verification of Stock and Property, Plant & Equipment (Fixed Assets), complete with detailed observation reports and executive summaries.
  • Certification: Providing professional certification of financial records and documents as required by authorities and financial institutions.
  • Forensic Audit: Specialized investigations into alleged frauds, irregularities, mismanagement, or actions taken beyond delegated powers.

Our Assurance Commitment

Professional endorsement is critical for transparency. We provide the meticulous oversight necessary to maintain regulatory standards and internal integrity.
