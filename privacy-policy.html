Privacy Policy

This policy explains, at a high level, how information shared through the Ganesan and Company website may be handled.

Information you share

If you contact us by email, phone, WhatsApp, or another communication channel, we may use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry and support a potential or existing professional engagement.

Website usage information

Basic technical information may be processed to maintain website security, performance, and reliability. We do not use this website to request sensitive financial or identity information through an unsecured form.

Sharing and retention

Information is handled only as reasonably necessary for responding to enquiries, providing services, meeting professional obligations, or complying with applicable law. Retention periods depend on the nature of the communication and our legal and professional responsibilities.

Contact
