The firm

A trusted partner for achieving excellence.

With humble beginnings in 1971, Ganesan and Company is now a highly valued audit, financial and management consultancy firm with over 50 years of experience. With offices at Pondicherry, Chennai and Bangalore, we are positioned to serve a diverse clientele across the Southern States of India.

Our extensive expertise spans traditional practice—including statutory and internal audits, taxation, MCA, FEMA and FCRA compliance—as well as new-age services such as Virtual CFO support, proactive performance evaluation and hand-holding compliance for start-ups.

Our seasoned professionals provide strategic insight and hands-on support, helping clients navigate financial complexity, minimize risk and build sustainable growth.

What we do

Our Work

As a forward-thinking firm, we are particularly interested in working with businesses which are in startup and Micro & Small Enterprises who are in initial stages in the growth trajectory. There are clients with an goal of tapping the capital market and are in requirement of Virtual CFO and other allied services. We are also interested in clients looking for compliance with various laws and regulations.

Our Values

Integrity, independence and diligence guide every engagement. We believe in transparent client relationships, rigorous technical standards and long-term partnerships built on trust rather than transactions.

Our Services

From statutory audit and taxation to Virtual CFO support and MCA/FEMA/FCRA compliance, we offer a full spectrum of financial services tailored to where your organisation is today and where it's headed next.
